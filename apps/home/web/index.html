A digital twin of your home’s energy

Describe your home, drop the files your electricity company and the weather service give you, and see what your home is made of: how fast it loses heat, how big your heating is, what is always on.

Your files never leave this computer. Everything happens inside this page: reading your files and fitting the model run in your browser. There is no server, and nothing is uploaded.
